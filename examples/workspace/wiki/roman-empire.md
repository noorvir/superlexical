# Roman Empire

The Roman Empire was the state that ruled the lands around the Mediterranean Sea from [Rome](rome.md). It began in 27 BC, when [Augustus](../lists/historical-figures/augustus.md) became its first emperor.

## Provinces

At its largest extent, in the second century, the empire reached from Britain to Mesopotamia. Its northern province of Britannia covered most of what is now [England](england.md).
